# Pioneer humanoid (final design)

URDF of the final Pioneer humanoid design: version 2 torso, armV2 arms, "hands D", and the wato legs. Units are
metres, Z is up, and the robot faces −Y.

- `pioneer_humanoid.urdf`: 70 links and 69 revolute joints:
  - a waist yaw and a 2-joint neck;
  - per arm, 5 joints plus a 2-joint wrist;
  - per hand, 20 finger joints, 4 of which (the DIP joints) mimic their PIP joint;
  - per leg, 6 joints (`Hip_F` pitch, `Hip_A` roll, `Hip_R` yaw, `Knee`, `Ankle_P`, `Ankle_R`).
- `meshes/`: one OBJ per link and colour (218 MB, in **Git LFS**: install `git-lfs` before cloning, or run
  `git lfs pull` after). Collision shapes are each link's bounding box.

Physics model, per-joint limits / torque / speed (`JOINT_LIMITS.md`) and a motor test tool:
`src/simulation/pioneer_humanoid_model/`.

## Corrections made here

Two joints had their limits on the impossible side. Their names and axes are unchanged:

| Joint | Original | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Knee_L`, `Knee_R` | −65..0° | **0..65°** | A positive angle bends the knee normally. The original range only bent it backwards, so the robot couldn't crouch, step or walk. The team's `whole_body_humanoid.urdf` has the same sign and should be checked too. |
| `l_elbow`, `r_elbow` | −90..90° | **−90..0°** | The positive half bent the elbow backwards. |

Every other joint was checked (what a positive angle does, and that both sides mirror each other).

## Placeholders

- **Masses and inertias** are estimated from CAD volume at 0.35 g/cm³, so the robot comes to 8 kg.
- **Every joint's effort and velocity** is 10 Nm and 6 rad/s.
- **Arm limits** are rough; shoulder pitch is ±180°.

The physics model overrides these with real or assumed values where they exist (`joint_specs.yaml` there).
