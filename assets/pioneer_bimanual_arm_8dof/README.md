# pioneer_bimanual_arm_8dof

The bimanual rig with its arms replaced by the **1107 Arm Draft** arms: two **7-DoF arms + a 1-DoF gripper** each.

- **Rig (`base_link`):** `pioneer_bimanual_arm`'s `base_link`, unchanged and referenced from that asset. That's the aluminium stand, steel shoulder bar, printed brackets, the two shoulder-pitch motors and the head camera.
- **Arms:** new, generated from the STEP. They hang off the same shoulder-pitch pivots as the old arms.

Generated by [`tools/bimanual_arm_8dof/build_bimanual_arm_8dof.py`](../../tools/bimanual_arm_8dof/build_bimanual_arm_8dof.py); don't edit the URDF by hand.

## Joints (both arms, same angles and signs)

| # | Joint | Motor | Limit (deg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `*_shoulder_pitch` | AK10 | −90 … +180 (+ = flexion, arm forward and up) |
| 2 | `*_shoulder_roll` | AK10 | −20 … +160 (+ = abduction, arm out) |
| 3 | `*_shoulder_yaw` | AK80 | −120 … +120 (upper-arm rotation) |
| 4 | `*_elbow` | AK80 | −5 … +130 (0 = straight, + = flexion) |
| 5 | `*_forearm_roll` | AK80 | −110 … +110 |
| 6 | `*_wrist_pitch` | AK45-10 | −60 … +60 |
| 7 | `*_wrist_yaw` | AK45-10 | −90 … +90 |
| — | `*_gripper` | AK40-10 | 0 … 0.041 m per finger (0 = open). `*_gripper_mimic`, the second finger, follows it. |

**Limits:** these are the owner's joint chart.

**Collision sweep** (`trimesh`/FCL on this model's visual meshes; each joint through its full range from the CAD pose; contacts already present in the CAD pose, i.e. mating faces, are ignored):
- **Shoulder pitch, shoulder roll (down to −20°), elbow, forearm roll, wrist pitch, wrist yaw:** clear over the full range.
- **Shoulder yaw:** from about **−78° to −100° (inward)**, with the elbow at 90°, the wrist (link6) hits the stand's centre post. That's a pose-dependent collision with the stand, not a joint limit. The IK doesn't avoid the post.
- **About the −9° roll contact:** the arm-shell session reported the roll bracket hitting at −9°. That sweep included its shell parts (pods, collars), and on the bare arm the roll is clear to −20°. The shell design is what limits the roll range.

**Zero pose:** the CAD pose (arms hanging, forearms forward), except the elbow, whose 0 is straight. The CAD pose is elbow = 90°, which is `DEFAULT_JOINT_POS` in `pioneer_humanoid.arm_8dof_params`.

**Right arm:** the left mirrored, with axes chosen so the same angle gives the mirror pose.

## Where the numbers come from

- **Geometry:** the STEP (`wilsonchenghy/Prometheus`, `Backend/cad_bench/projects/arm_shell/source/arm_draft_1107.step.xz`).
- **Joint axes and pivots:** found from the bearing and motor geometry (arm-shell session, `arm_shell.py JOINTS`). The STEP has no joints.
- **Base frame:** the old arm's `base_link` frame, from the arm-shell session's draft-to-URDF mapping. The pitch motors coincide.
- **Masses: estimates.** The STEP has no mass properties. Each link's mass is spread over its convex hull: link1 1.15, link2 0.75, link3 0.85, link4 0.75, link5 0.45, link6 0.40, link7 0.40, fingers 0.04 kg. Replace them with measured values.
- **Collision:** one convex hull per link. Visual meshes are decimated to about 6k faces.

## Regenerate

```bash
pip install build123d trimesh fast_simplification   # build123d brings OCP (the STEP reader)
xz -dk arm_draft_1107.step.xz
python tools/bimanual_arm_8dof/build_bimanual_arm_8dof.py arm_draft_1107.step assets/pioneer_bimanual_arm_8dof
```

## Used by
- **MuJoCo:** `humanoid_mujoco_scenes.make_model(scene)`, where it is the default robot (`robot="armv2"` gives the old arms), and `pioneer_humanoid.mujoco_bimanual_arm_8dof`.
- **Quest teleop in MuJoCo:** `--robot 8dof`, the default.
- **Isaac:** `pioneer_humanoid.bimanual_arm_8dof.BIMANUAL_ARM_8DOF_CFG`. It converts the URDF to USD on first spawn (about 1 min) and caches it in `usd/` (gitignored: the USD is 114 MB, over GitHub's file limit), with the URDF colours. Link poses, limits, masses and PD gains match the MuJoCo model; the gripper's back-drive friction (`GRIPPER_FRICTIONLOSS`) is MuJoCo only.
- **Not yet ported:** the leader-arm teleop, policy evaluation, and the real-arm stack. These stay on `pioneer_bimanual_arm`.
