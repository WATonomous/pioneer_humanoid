Pioneer VR teleoperation

Quest 2 + Touch controllers · simulated Pioneer bimanual arms

Connecting…

Tracked controllers move the arms automatically.
Index trigger: squeeze to close the gripper. A / X: hold for precision motion.
Left Y: reset scene. Thumbstick click: hold to pause that arm and calibrate the wrist pivot.

Tracking loss pauses movement. Tracking return resumes from the current arm pose. Forward is aligned to your headset heading when tracking engages.
