PioneerNet results

A 12M-parameter RGB-D ResNet-18, distilled from DINOv2 and DeFM, gives the best clean robot policy we have tested, frozen, and the most robust one under lighting, colour, fog, glare, lens and shadow changes. v5 failed a new background (8%); v6, trained with random background panels and distractors, reaches 32%, and 24% on a held-out tablecloth where every baseline gets 0–6%.

The scenarios

Light and colour changes leave depth untouched, so these also hit depth where a real sensor would: fog and glare cause depth dropout, a wet lens blanks depth under the drops, and clutter and a new background change the geometry. Shadows change colour only, as in reality. The tablecloth was added after v6's training corruptions were designed and before any v6 result, so it is a held-out test for v6.
